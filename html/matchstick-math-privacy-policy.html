Privacy Policy

Matchstick Math - Last updated: April 30, 2026

Matchstick Math is a completely offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

Matchstick Math does not collect any personal information. We do not collect:

  • Personal identification data (name, email, phone number)
  • Device information (device ID, IMEI, IP address)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers

Data Storage

All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device using the device's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

Matchstick Math does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The app functions entirely offline.

Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from children or adults. We comply with COPPA and other children's privacy regulations by simply not collecting any data at all.

Data Sharing

Since we do not collect any data, we do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

Data Security

As no data is collected or transmitted, there are no data security risks associated with this app.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy in the future, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Any changes that affect data collection practices will be clearly communicated.
